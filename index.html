What does the score mean?

The score measures how “anisotropic” (directionally coherent) the gradient field is. Real photos often have more coherent gradients tied to physical edges/lighting. Diffusion images often show more unstable high-frequency gradient structure. You should calibrate the threshold using known real/AI samples.

Tip: Use multiple images for calibration; don’t rely on a single score.
